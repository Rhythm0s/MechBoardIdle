```
WHERE THE FEET SIT IN THE PICTURE: the two feet are never at the same height on screen.
Through the first half of the cycle the LEFT foot is the lower one in the picture and the
right foot sits higher and further back. Through the second half they have traded: the RIGHT
foot is the lower one and the left foot sits higher and further back. They swap which one is
lower exactly once per cycle, and in the middle frames the two feet pass close together near
the same height.
```

```

the machine's height. They are never level.

THE LEGS PASS EACH OTHER: as they trade, the lifted leg swings in front of the standing leg
and its shin overlaps the other leg's shin for one frame, so the two legs visibly cross.
```

**「4분의 1」이 장치다** — 「같은 높이가 아니다」만으로는 **한 화소 차이도 만족**이라 눈에 안 보인다.
